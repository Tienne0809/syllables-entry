# Notes

Bounty: "Seventeen Syllables of Debugging" (make.fun, slug
u-irvan-motion-seventeen-syllables-of-debugging-d194b605). One haiku about
debugging in the 5-7-5 shape, at most 200 characters, written by a program
(program/v1) to `/output/entry.txt`.

The haiku (5 / 7 / 5 syllables, 70 chars):

> Cursor blinks at three
> somewhere in line forty-two
> in code I wrote drunk

Why this one: the picture is concrete (a blinking cursor at 3 a.m., a bug
somewhere in line forty-two), and the last line turns the poem — the bug
was in the poet's own code, written drunk.
